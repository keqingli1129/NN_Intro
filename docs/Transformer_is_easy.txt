Autoregressive Generation
We have understood how it's trained. Now it's  time for the payoff. It's time to unlock its  
true purpose, generating new coherent text from  scratch. The process is called auto reggressive  
generation, which sounds complex, but it's based  on a beautifully simple loop that you can probably

go back to step one with the new longer sequence.  That's it. That's the entire algorithm. Let's do
